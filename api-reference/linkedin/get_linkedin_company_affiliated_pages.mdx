---
title: "LinkedIn Company Affiliated Pages"
openapi: "GET /linkedin/company/affiliated-pages"
---

Read the visible affiliated-page cards for a numeric LinkedIn company ID. Includes company, school and showcase links, preserving their page types. This is a bounded public window, not a complete ownership tree. Related numeric IDs, total and continuation remain unknown. A successful live request costs 5 credits; cached responses and idempotent replays cost zero.
