---
title: "LinkedIn Company by Domain"
openapi: "GET /linkedin/company/by-domain"
---

Look up one indexed LinkedIn company by its bare public website domain. The returned company website must match that domain or a subdomain. This is not an exhaustive domain-to-subsidiary search. Employee and follower counts remain source-reported with unknown precision. Supply domain without a URL scheme or path. A successful live request costs 5 credits; cached responses and idempotent replays cost zero.

[Source contract](https://docs.piloterr.com/linkedin-company-info).
