---
title: "Klarna Product"
openapi: "GET /klarna/product"
---

Look up a US Klarna product by its complete canonical product URL. Product-ID-only lookup and other regions are unavailable and return 422. Optional product_id and category_id must match the URL. Returns source product identity and retailer offers. Merchant links, individual stock status, conditions, ratings and descriptions are unavailable. Retailers can repeat for distinct variants; these are retained. The source minimum in-stock price is separate from the lowest listed offer price, whose stock status is unknown.

A successful uncached request costs **5 credits**. Failed requests and cache hits cost zero. Empty offers are valid only when the source returns a matching product record. No price-history, review or all-region coverage is implied.
