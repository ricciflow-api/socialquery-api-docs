---
title: "Yelp Business Info"
openapi: "GET /yelp/business/info"
---

Retrieve public Yelp business details using a 22-character business ID, or a canonical yelp.com/biz URL containing that ID. Business aliases are not accepted. Returns contact details, rating, review totals, photos, source-text hours and features. Current open/closed status is not inferred.

Successful uncached requests cost **1 credits**. Cache hits and failed requests cost zero. Provider availability and free allowance may change.
