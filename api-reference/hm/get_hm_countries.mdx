---
title: "H&M Countries"
openapi: "GET /hm/countries"
---

Read the current [H&M market selector](https://www.hm.com/entrance.ahtml), grouped by region and preserving its language choices. This endpoint takes no query parameters and does not require a separate provider account.

`regions[].countries[]` contains the market name, its two-letter `locale`, and `languages`. Each language includes its source label, language code, `storefront_locale` such as `en_us`, and an observed H&M `url`. These URLs select a market and may redirect to a regional storefront; they are not guessed storefront addresses.

The response reports `total_countries`, the source URL and a coverage note. Presence in this selector does not prove that a country has online delivery, stock or a working stores API. H&M's country-directory and catalog-language codes differ.

Malformed or blocked source pages return an error, never a fabricated empty directory. Cached responses and idempotent replays are free; fresh successful calls use the endpoint's documented credit price.

SocialQuery retrieves this public HTML through its configured Firecrawl provider, using a fresh basic scrape. Direct requests from Vercel failed the initial production check and are not used by the deployed adapter. Source trial capacity remains finite.
