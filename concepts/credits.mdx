title: Credits
description: Estimate a workload and reconcile its actual credit usage.
---

Signup adds 100 trial credits. Check the operation reference for the charge applied to a successful live request. Qualifying cache hits consume no credits, and failed provider calls do not leave a charge.

When the balance is insufficient, HTTP 402 is returned before contacting the source. Use `GET /v1/credits/balance` to inspect available credits and the dashboard ledger to review their movements.

The pricing page describes one-time credit packs. Check the dashboard for current checkout availability; viewing a pack does not create a purchase, subscription, or automatic recharge.
